# Android 2.3.0

- versionName: 2.3.0
- versionCode: 2003000
- Native Bridge: v2 (unchanged)
- Minimum supported version: unchanged at 2002002 (2.2.2).

Minor release for realistic 3D replay combat effects in the shared frontend: shells fly as team-coloured tracers, each shot leaves a thin team-coloured trail that holds for 2.5 s and fades over 1.5 s on the replay clock, guns fire with a muzzle flash, and hits show a small flash with smoke while keeping penetration, non-penetration and ricochet visually distinct. Vehicle destruction becomes a full explosion (flash, fireball, smoke column and sparks, plus debris and ground dust on higher tiers), and wrecks keep smoking on High/Ultra. Effect detail follows the existing four quality tiers, with all particles batched under a fixed per-tier capacity. 2D replay is unchanged. Builds on 2.2.11. Stage builds and verifies the immutable APK and evidence; production version.json changes only through a separate publish run.
