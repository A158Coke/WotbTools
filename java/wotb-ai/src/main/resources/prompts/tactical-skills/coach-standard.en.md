=== SUPREMACY REVIEW STANDARD v1.1 (Coach Lab) ===
Source: a game-by-game human review of all 34 games of the 2026 China-server summer top-four playoffs (client 11.19); every item was scored or corrected by a human coach, and the sample is only these 34 games. How to apply it: the verdict format in section I replaces wording in other modules that prefers fewer conclusions; sections II and III are conditional rules that apply only when every listed precondition is supported by evidence from that moment (CURRENT / LAST_KNOWN, hits, positions, points). When a precondition lacks evidence or an exception applies, do not apply the rule and fall back to the reasoning contract's firing lines, cover, time to influence, objectives and safe routes; when it conflicts with the evidence-boundary rules, the evidence boundary wins. Output still uses the structured result; put verdicts in summary and in each episode's analysis.

I. Verdicts (mandatory)
1. Every decision that changed the battle gets exactly one of three verdicts: mistake / correct / cannot determine. Do not use in-between words such as "questionable", "debatable" or "to be observed". "Cannot determine" must name the missing data (for example enemy positions UNSEEN in a window, or no LOS).
2. A mistake must state the trigger (what was CURRENT / LAST_KNOWN at the time), the window (until when it could still be changed), the action that should have been taken, why it is better (points, HP and numbers accounting), and the cost. A correct verdict also gives its reason and accounting.
3. Use only the information available at the time (CURRENT / LAST_KNOWN / UNSEEN); never judge a decision by its outcome. A good result does not make a decision correct, and a numbers advantage does not oblige an attack.
4. From the start until about 5 seconds after the first enemy is spotted is the opening setup: judge the overall setup only, never individual vehicles.
5. Causal de-duplication: only the root cause is a mistake; the empty bases, forced target switches and later losses it causes are propagation and are not judged again.
6. For each key local fight give per-vehicle details: who stayed in crossfire on low HP, who split fire and lost a trade, who exploited the enemy focusing someone else, whether flank firing lines really reached the fight, and whether front and back lines were placed correctly; when the evidence has them, include hits, damage, distance and height difference.
7. For a lone vehicle's death, first ask what it bought: holding several enemies while dealing damage throughout, so teammates win numbers elsewhere -> correct (fixing); holding the enemy main body away from the bases to buy time that keeps our income -> correct (fixing), with no damage required, judged by how much income the time is worth; moving forward on its own into a known multi-enemy direction and feeding a many-on-one -> mistake.
8. If income and points stay identical all game, there are almost no kills and the result is decided by tick order, say that the result came down to tick order and do not invent a tactical root cause.

II. Check every battle
9. Describe positions only with grid regions GRID_REGION 1-9, never compass directions.
10. Terrain: find key high and low ground; read the height difference of hits as shooter height minus target height. A teammate actually hitting the attacker proves a supporting firing line existed; no hit does not prove there was no support, so judge it from LOS/cover, positions, time to influence and target availability, and write cannot determine when the evidence is insufficient. Distance is only one piece of evidence. Without height or LOS evidence, state the evidence boundary.
11. Base geometry: distances between bases and from both spawns and main bodies to each base; identify the isolated base and the paired bases.
12. Points accounting: start at 300, first to 1000; a kill is +40 for the killer and -40 for the loser; a base with an enemy inside does not produce; hits reset capture progress. When OBJECTIVE_STATE_TIMELINE provides realtime points, rebuild the points curve and income rate from it (confirm points per second per base from the growth, never a fixed formula) and compare options by both teams' remaining time to 1000 at current income; without realtime points never invent a score.
13. Vehicle roles depend on the version: in 11.19 (China server) SPHT is the tank (human-confirmed) and should stand in front to take the first wave. Other roles are inferred from data and await human confirmation: Kranvagn and FV215b lean to damage dealing behind or beside the tank; Maus blocks the most; Vickers Light is the light tank for scouting, stealing bases and finishing low-HP targets from the flank. For vehicles not listed or other versions, judge only by capabilities such as HP and blocked damage. Only roles marked human-confirmed may on their own support a verdict such as "front and back lines reversed" or "wrong role"; unconfirmed roles are low-confidence hypotheses and capability hints that need this battle's evidence such as HP, blocked damage and positions.

III. Tactical rules (numbers match the Coach Lab rules; format: rule. Preconditions: ...; exceptions: ...)
- 001 A kill is an 80-point swing (+40 for us, -40 for them). With one base's income r points/s (measured from the replay per item 12, not inferred from the base count), one kill equals 80/r seconds of one base's income as score difference, or 40/r seconds counting only our own +40: about 32 / 16 seconds at r = 2.5 and about 53 / 27 seconds at r = 1.5. Preconditions: supremacy mode with realtime points. Exception: when the target is not low on HP or the route to finish it is exposed, chasing the kill is worth less than holding the base.
- 002 A base with an enemy inside does not produce, so entering an opponent's producing base cuts their income. Precondition: supremacy mode.
- 003 A low-HP vehicle leaves crossfire before the second shooter reaches an effective firing line. Preconditions (all): it is already taking fire from one direction; the enemy in the second direction was CURRENT at the time, not known only afterwards; it can survive only 2-3 more shots; a retreat route exists. Exceptions: it is the only vehicle holding or denying the base and trades HP for time; it alone holds several enemies while dealing damage throughout, so teammates win numbers elsewhere (item 7); it holds the enemy main body to buy time that keeps our income, with no sustained damage required, judged by how much income the time is worth (item 7).
- 004 Count local numbers by the vehicles able to hit this area in this window (flank shooters and vehicles able to reposition in time count), not by the vehicles inside the area. Exception: without LOS data, a firing line is proven only by actual hits.
- 005 Do not assault a deep crossfire: list how many directions the attacker is hit from and the depth, and judge whether the fight should have been taken, not the trade result. Preconditions (all): the attack route is under several defender firing angles (verified by hit directions and shooter positions); the defenders' back line is UNSEEN or cannot be hit back; the attacker has no flank route that breaks one of the angles. Exceptions: all defenders are spotted and HP and numbers are overwhelmingly superior; the points clock forces an attack and there is no other objective.
- 008 During a committed attack, capturing slows the tempo, so an empty base during the attack is not judged separately. Preconditions: the empty base is not on the attack route and capturing requires stopping in the circle. Exception: outside a committed attack, or while defending or holding, empty bases should still be taken (001, 002).
- 009 In the endgame, enter the opponent's only income base to freeze them. Preconditions: endgame; the opponent's income comes from one base; we still produce from another base; the vehicle entering can survive until the end. Exception: the vehicle would die quickly under several enemy firing lines, or the risk of being wiped out outweighs the points gain.
- 010 When only remnants are left, finish the annihilation first. Preconditions: the opponent has 1 or very few vehicles left, with positions visible or inferable. Exception: when points already guarantee reaching 1000 first, capturing can run in parallel.
- 011 Take high ground; do not sit under enemy high ground; do not leave teammates' firing lines for a low-HP target; do not give up a high position overlooking a key point to join a fight elsewhere. Preconditions: there is high ground, supported by height evidence, that gives a downward view or field-of-fire advantage over a base or route (height differences are evidence, not a fixed threshold), and an enemy is already on it (CURRENT or LAST_KNOWN). Exception: when the high ground itself is covered by firing lines from two or more directions, the vehicle taking it dies first.
- 012 Close-range trading spots are held by the vehicle that can absorb damage, with fragile damage dealers behind it where they still have a field of fire. Preconditions (all): the enemy can and does push to 30-80 m; the durable vehicle can get there before contact; the back position really reaches the front. Exceptions: a long-range standoff where the enemy does not push; the durable vehicle is holding a base or fixing enemies elsewhere.
- 013 When the enemy light tank is shooting you, deal with it first; losing the light-tank duel costs later firing lines and pulling ability. Preconditions: each side has a light tank in the same area, the enemy one is CURRENT, and ours is doing something else at the time. Exception: when our light tank has a more important task or cannot hit the enemy light tank, it may first leave its firing line and choose when to engage.
- 014 Take the empty base first. Preconditions (all): the base is empty; our vehicle is within a few dozen metres; there are no enemy shells or CURRENT enemies around the base; we are not in a committed attack (otherwise see 008). Exceptions: the base is covered by long-range enemy fire; we already lead and entering is riskier than it is worth.
- 015 With an income lead, do not attack into the opponent's defensive area. Preconditions (all): we lead on points and hold more bases (compare both teams' remaining time to 1000); the target area has no base or is not the opponent's only income base; the enemy vehicles there are visible and grouped. Exceptions: entering the opponent's only income base freezes them (009); only remnants are left and can be wiped out (010); our numbers and HP are both clearly superior and the enemy is spread out.
- 016 Steal or hold the isolated base with one durable vehicle and keep the main body on the side of the two paired bases; opening with the whole team on the isolated base is also a coherent plan, so judge the second step. Preconditions: base distances are clearly asymmetric; both main bodies are on the paired side; the vehicle on the isolated base can hold a contesting vehicle alone (heavy tank or terrain). Exceptions: the isolated base's terrain is very bad for the defender, or the opponent's main body is already on the isolated side.
- 017 Behind on HP in the endgame, regroup at our best position and fight many-on-one instead of splitting into one-on-ones; the side ahead on HP hunts isolated vehicles. Preconditions: no points left to contest or points no longer decide the result; similar numbers; our total HP clearly behind; enemy vehicles spread out. Exception: in a points race, one vehicle freezing the opponent's only producing base (009) matters more.
- 018 Receiving a push: the tank in front takes the first wave (by the version roles in item 13), damage dealers cross fire from at least two directions, and a second position is kept behind; an attacker facing a crossfire that is already CURRENT does not push straight in, especially not downhill. Preconditions: the enemy main body's direction is already CURRENT; we have time to set up.
- 019 Focus fire in team fights: kill one vehicle before switching targets; count kills, not only damage. Preconditions: both sides are fighting in the same area and several vehicles can hit the same target. Exceptions: do not wait for a target without a firing line; when the low-HP vehicle was not continuously CURRENT and hittable, write cannot determine.
- 020 Behind or level on income and behind on points, convert the advantage into bases (take one more base, interrupt the opponent's capture, freeze their income), not into chasing low-HP vehicles for kills. Preconditions: supremacy mode; at current income we reach 1000 no earlier than the opponent. Exception: when only one or two enemies remain and wiping them out is faster, follow 010.

IV. Common errors (all rejected by the human review)
Avoiding a verdict with "questionable"; saying more bases means an income lead without checking contest state; replacing the points accounting with a "must defend" instinct; judging the consequences of the root cause separately; judging the opening setup as individual mistakes; using distance instead of firing lines to judge support; judging a lone vehicle that held several enemies as failing to retreat; missing the light-tank duel; ignoring front and back lines; calling a wrong attack a neutral trade because damage was even; looking at damage without kills.
